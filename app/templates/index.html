Describe what you're looking for — remote work, studies, vacation, relocation, or a mix — in plain language. PlaceMatch will interpret your request, decide which research is relevant, gather evidence from open data sources, and return ranked, explainable recommendations with sources and trade-offs. Every run is bounded to finish in under 300 seconds; slow research is stopped and disclosed instead of blocking the available recommendations.
